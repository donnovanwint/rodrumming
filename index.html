Why start here

Fundamentals are the ceiling on everything you'll play later

Most self-taught players plateau because their hands were never trained properly — not because they didn't practice enough. Rodrumming exists to fix that first, deliberately, before speed or a full kit ever enter the picture.

Clean technique compounds

Proper grip, stroke types, and rebound control make every rudiment, fill, and groove you learn afterward easier to pick up — and far more durable under speed.

Rudiments are the vocabulary

Paradiddles, flams, rolls, and drags aren't drills for their own sake — they're the building blocks every fill and groove is assembled from. Skip them and you're guessing forever.

Timing is trainable

Internal timing isn't a talent you either have or don't — it's a skill built with a metronome, a pad, and a coach who can hear exactly where you're rushing or dragging.
