Teams' default noise suppression is built for cancelling background noise, so it can flatten or mute the transient sound of sticks hitting a pad — which makes it harder for me to hear your playing clearly. Turn it off before we start:

Before the call: Open Teams → click your profile picture → Settings → Devices → scroll to Noise suppression → set it to Off (or Low if Off isn't available on your version).

During the call: Click the More (•••) menu in your meeting controls → Settings → Manage noise suppression → select Off.
