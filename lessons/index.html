Lessons & Approach

One-on-one, live over video, built around fundamentals.

No kit, no ensemble curriculum, no wasted time. Just focused work on sticking technique, rudiments, and timing — with me watching and correcting in real time.

Format

What a lesson actually looks like

Video call, face to face

Lessons run live over Zoom (or your preferred video platform). I can see your hands, hear your stick control, and correct technique in the moment — same as being in the room.

Pad-based, not kit-based

Everything happens on a practice pad or similar surface. That's intentional — it isolates hand technique so nothing about a kit setup gets in the way of the correction.

Focused drill work

Each session targets a specific piece of technique or rudiment, worked at a controlled tempo with a metronome, then built up gradually — not rushed.

A practice plan to take away

You leave every lesson with a clear, specific plan for what to drill before the next one — so progress compounds between sessions, not just during them.

What you'll need

Almost nothing to get started

That's the point. Rodrumming is designed to be accessible without any kit investment up front.

  • A pair of drumsticks Any standard pair works — I can advise on sizing in your first lesson.
  • A practice pad or firm surface A dedicated pad is ideal, but a pillow, phone book, or snare-height surface works to start.
  • A laptop, tablet, or phone with a camera Positioned so I can see your hands and stick angle clearly.
  • Stable internet for video calling Zoom is standard; other video platforms can be arranged.
  • No drum kit required These lessons are fundamentals-focused — kit and ensemble playing aren't part of the curriculum.

Who it's for

From first-time players to working drummers cleaning up their hands

Complete beginners

Never held sticks before? Lessons start with grip, posture, and basic strokes — no prior experience needed.

Self-taught players

Already play but never learned properly? Sessions target the specific habits holding your technique back.

Intermediate & advanced players

Want to tighten rudiments, clean up rebound control, or sharpen internal timing? Lessons go as deep as your level requires.
