No drum kit. That's intentional.

We don't use a drum set in lessons. The focus is building the fundamentals that prepare you to play a kit, march a drumline, or audition for a drum corps with control and confidence.

Why fundamentals first?

Great drummers aren't born behind a kit or in a drumline — they're built on a strong foundation.

The Building Blocks

Grip, stroke, and timing are the core skills every great drummer relies on.

Everything Transfers

These skills show up in fills, grooves, marching cadences, and timekeeping. The pad is where it starts.

Faster Progress

Focused, intentional practice leads to cleaner technique and fewer bad habits.

Ready to Advance

You'll move to a kit, marching band, or drum corps with a solid foundation and a clear understanding of your skills.

What You'll Learn

The Rudiments

We work through the standard vocabulary of drumming — the rudiments every great player builds fluency, control, and creativity from:

  • Proper grip and stroke technique
  • Single stroke, double stroke, and beyond
  • Rolls, drags, and dynamic control
  • Coordination and independence
  • Timing, rhythm, and feel
  • Application to real playing
40 Rudiments A Complete Foundation

These skills will help you:

  • Play with better time and feel
  • Develop cleaner technique
  • Gain control at any tempo
  • Express more with less effort
  • Build confidence on any surface
  • Transition to a kit, marching ensemble, or drum corps with confidence
